What the numbers are. EPS 2027 = Bloomberg blended-calendar consensus for the calendar year 2027 — a fixed period, which is what makes the line a revision path rather than a rolling blend (1BF/2BF roll forward with the calendar and would mix growth into the slope). Re-rating = (1+price)÷(1+EPS revision)−1, measured between the same two weeks as the other two, so it equals the change in the card's own CY2027 P/E.
Windows are not identical. Consensus for a year that far out builds up over time: 34 of the 36 names have a CY2027 number from Jan/Feb-2025, but AGX only from Jun-2025 and AAOI from Nov-2025. Each card starts at its own first week with both a price and an estimate — flagged on the card when that is later than Feb-2025 — so a sort by revision is comparing across different windows. The 3m column in the table view is the like-for-like read.
Gaps. Weeks where BEst published no CY2027 value are left as gaps and the line spans them; the card reports how many, and carries a ⚠ when more than a third of the window is interpolated (AGX, ENR GY, AAOI, CIEN, CSCO, COHR, LITE, AAPL — for those the slope between points is drawn, not observed). Names whose CY2027 EPS is negative or near zero are left out of this tab (indexing to a base near zero is meaningless) — they are listed at the bottom if any.
